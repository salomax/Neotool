# ADR Template

Purpose: standard shape for new Architecture Decision Records under `docs/04-decisions/`.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected

## Context

What forces the decision? Keep to verified constraints.

## Decision

State the choice in imperative language. One decision per ADR.

## Consequences

Positive, negative, and follow-up obligations.

## Evidence

List code/config/test paths that prove the decision is (or is not) in force.
