# Initiative Brief · Pilot booking for one shared meeting room

- **ID / name:** INIT-01 · Pilot booking for one shared meeting room
- **State:** Draft
- **Owner:** Workplace Operations
- **Summary:** Explore whether staff can make reliable reservations for one shared meeting room.
- **Strategy link:** Advances [OBJ-01 · Reduce scheduling conflicts](product-strategy.md#obj-01--reduce-scheduling-conflicts).

## Situation and intended outcome

Staff need to know whether they can rely on a reservation. They currently book through chat and have reported conflicting confirmations. The initiative focuses on one room and aims to reduce confirmed overlaps.

## What we know and need to learn

| ID | Kind | Statement or question | Source or how to learn more |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OBS-1 | Observation | Staff reported conflicting confirmations. The frequency is unknown. | [Booking example](first-delivery.md) |
| HYP-1 | Hypothesis | Showing availability earlier could reduce conflicts. | Compare the pilot with the baseline if comparable data is available. |
| Q-1 | Open question | Do the chat confirmations give us a usable baseline? | Check whether the room, time interval, and confirmation status can be established. |

## Next move

- **Activity:** Explore the problem.
- **Question:** Can we answer Q-1 for the pilot room?
- **Why now:** The answer determines whether the existing confirmations can serve as the baseline for OBJ-01.
- **Inputs and approach:** Review five office days of chat confirmations for the room and record overlaps, total confirmed reservations, and gaps.
- **Expected result and stopping condition:** A baseline with counts for the five-day window, or a list of gaps and a proposal for collecting a comparable baseline before the pilot.
- **Owner and state:** Workplace Operations · Agreed.
