# Product Strategy · Shared office booking

This fictional example shows an agreed strategy with a baseline and target still to be established.

- **ID:** `strategy.shared-office-booking`
- **Kind:** Product strategy
- **State:** Agreed
- **Owner:** Workplace Operations
- **Summary:** Make self-service booking reliable for small teams using shared office resources.

## Vision

Staff at small offices can count on having a shared room when their reservation says they do.

## Strategic choices

Start with a single shared room and immediate feedback on availability. Defer calendar integration until the pilot shows whether immediate feedback helps. The [booking example](first-delivery.md) records reports of overlapping chat confirmations and identifies the booking form as an assumption to evaluate.

## OBJ-01 · Reduce scheduling conflicts

- **Kind:** Objective
- **State:** Agreed
- **Outcome:** Fewer confirmed overlapping reservations for the pilot room.
- **Measure and window:** Compare the share of confirmed reservations that overlap another confirmed reservation across five office days before the pilot and five office days during it. Record counts and denominators for both periods.
- **Baseline and target:** Baseline pending. Workplace Operations will review chat confirmations for the pre-pilot window and agree on a target before the pilot begins.
- **Rationale and sources:** This objective reflects the [strategic choice](#strategic-choices) and the duplicate bookings described in the [worked example](first-delivery.md).

## Open questions

If chat records are incomplete, Workplace Operations will agree on how to collect a comparable baseline before drawing conclusions from the pilot.
