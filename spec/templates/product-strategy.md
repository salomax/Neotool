# Product Strategy

- **ID:** `strategy.<slug>`
- **Kind:** Product strategy
- **State:** Draft / agreed / superseded
- **Owner:** Person or team responsible for strategic choices
- **Summary:** The audience, focus, and improvement we seek in one sentence

## Vision

Describe who the product serves and what should improve for them.

## Strategic choices

State where to focus, what is outside this choice, and why. Link to the source or label the assumption behind it. Capture alternatives only where they explain the choice.

## OBJ-01 · Outcome name

Use a stable ID and link to this heading from the Product Map or an initiative. Repeat this section for additional objectives.

- **Kind:** Objective
- **State:** Proposed / agreed / superseded
- **Outcome:** The change we intend to observe
- **Measure and window:** How and when we will assess it
- **Baseline and target:** Record the values or mark what is unknown and how to obtain it
- **Rationale and sources:** Link to the strategic choice and the relevant evidence

## Open questions

Record gaps that could change a choice or how progress is assessed. For each gap, state what would resolve it.
